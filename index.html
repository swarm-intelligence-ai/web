I work with executives and organisations to cut through complexity and turn data, AI, and transformation into measurable business outcomes.

I'm outcome-driven, people-focused, and pragmatic in execution. I'm most interested in working on problems where there's real complexity and something meaningful to fix. Where clarity, alignment, and delivery actually matter.
