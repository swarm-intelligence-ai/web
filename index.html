03Design and governance framework

Insight people can understand, trust and act on.

The Swarm Insights Design & Governance Framework is a practical path from evidence to decision-ready insight. It brings information design, accessibility, human review and clear ownership together, for analytics and AI alike.

  1. 01

    Evidence

    Questions, data and the context needed to interpret them.

  2. 02

    Design

    Insight and interaction shaped around the decision.

  3. 03

    Assure

    Human review and traceability where it matters.

  4. 04

    Govern

    Named ownership and decision rights that outlast the project.
