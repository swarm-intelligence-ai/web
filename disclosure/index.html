Affiliate disclosure

Last updated 28 September 2026

Swarm Intelligence earns revenue from consulting services and from Swarm Labs apps. This page explains how partner offers in our apps work.

Partner offers in our apps

Some Swarm Labs apps will include rewards and offers from selected partners. If you follow an offer and buy something or sign up, we may receive a commission from that partner. It doesn’t change the price you pay or the terms you’re offered.

We work with partners directly and through affiliate networks.

How offers are labelled

  • Offers are always labelled as partner offers inside the app.
  • They appear alongside the app’s own features, never in place of them or disguised as advice.
  • We only include offers we’d consider relevant and reasonable for the people using that app.

Independence

Commercial relationships don’t decide how our apps work or what they show you about your own information. We don’t accept payment to present a product as a recommendation.

This website

This website doesn’t contain affiliate links or advertising.
