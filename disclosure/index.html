Affiliate disclosure and editorial standards

Last updated 25 September 2026

How Swarm Intelligence earns money, how we handle affiliate links, and the standards our guides follow.

How we earn money

Swarm Intelligence Pty Ltd earns revenue from consulting services and from Swarm Labs products. Some Swarm Labs guides and product pages may also include affiliate links, which can earn us a commission.

What an affiliate link is

An affiliate link lets a provider know that you came from our website. If you follow one and buy something or sign up, we may receive a commission from that provider. It doesn’t change the price you pay or the terms you’re offered.

We may take part in affiliate programs run directly by providers, or through affiliate networks such as Commission Factory and Awin. These networks may use cookies or similar technology on their own websites to attribute a referral. See our privacy policy.

How we label them

  • Any page that contains affiliate links says so near the top, before the first link.
  • Each guide states at the end whether it contains affiliate links.
  • When we mention one of our own Swarm Labs products, it’s clearly identified as ours.

As at the date above, our guides contain no affiliate links.

Editorial independence

Our guides are written to be useful whether or not you ever follow a link or use one of our apps. Commercial relationships don’t decide what we cover, what we conclude or how we describe a product. We don’t accept payment for positive coverage, and we only link to products or services we’d consider reasonable for the reader a guide is written for.

Money content

Money guides are general information, not personal financial advice. Where we mention a type of financial product, such as a savings account, it’s to help you compare, not a recommendation that a particular product suits your circumstances.

Health content

Health guides are general organisational information, not medical advice. We don’t promote prescription medicines, and we don’t make claims about treatment outcomes.

Sources and review

Guides are checked against official Australian sources, such as Moneysmart, the Fair Work Ombudsman, the Australian Taxation Office and healthdirect, and link to the sources they rely on. Each guide shows when it was last updated. We review guides when official guidance changes, and at least once a year.

Corrections

If you think something we’ve published is wrong or out of date, email anthony@swarmintelligence.au. We’ll review it and correct it where needed.
