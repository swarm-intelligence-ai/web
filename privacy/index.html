Privacy policy

Last updated 25 September 2026

This policy explains how Swarm Intelligence Pty Ltd (ABN 80 136 699 477) handles personal information on swarmintelligence.au and in Swarm Labs products. We aim to handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

In short

  • This website doesn’t use analytics or advertising cookies, and doesn’t set cookies of its own.
  • We only receive personal information you choose to send us, such as an email enquiry.
  • We don’t sell personal information.
  • If you follow an affiliate link, the affiliate network and the provider may set cookies on their own websites to attribute the referral.
  • Dosely is designed so your medication diary stays on your iPhone.

This website

You can read this website without giving us any personal information. It doesn’t use analytics tools, advertising cookies or tracking pixels, and our fonts and images are served from our own website rather than third-party services.

Like any website, our hosting provider automatically processes technical information such as IP addresses, browser type, pages requested and timestamps, to deliver the site and protect it from misuse. We don’t use this information to identify you.

If we introduce measurement tools in future, we’ll update this policy before doing so and favour privacy-respecting options.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive what you include, such as your name, email address, organisation and the content of your enquiry. We use it to respond, to keep appropriate business records and to manage any resulting relationship. If you register interest in a Swarm Labs product, we use your email address only to tell you about that product.

Affiliate links and cookies

Some pages may include affiliate links, which are always disclosed on the page. When you click one, you leave our website. The affiliate network (such as Commission Factory or Awin) and the provider you visit may use cookies or similar technology to record that the visit came from us, so a commission can be attributed. Those cookies are set by them, not by us, and are governed by their privacy policies. Read our affiliate disclosure for more detail.

Swarm Labs products

Swarm Labs products are in development. Each product’s privacy information will be finalised and published before it’s released. The principles below describe how each one is being designed.

Money Money Money

Money Money Money is in testing. This website doesn’t collect financial information for the app. Full product privacy information, including what the app stores and where, will be published before release.

Defrazzle

Defrazzle is in development. It’s designed around notices a person deliberately chooses to share, a strong preference for on-device processing, and review by a parent or carer before anything is saved. This website doesn’t accept family notices.

Dosely

Dosely is in early development and is designed as a local-only medication diary. Diary information is stored in the app’s private storage on your iPhone with iOS file protection. The product is designed without advertising or third-party analytics.

You can export your data as a PDF report or a raw data file, enable optional Face ID, Touch ID or passcode access, and delete your diary. Once you share or save an exported file outside Dosely, that copy is controlled by the destination you chose and is no longer protected by the app.

How we use and disclose information

We use personal information for the purpose you provided it, and for related purposes you’d reasonably expect. We may use service providers, such as email and hosting providers, to operate our business, and we may disclose information where required by law. Some of these providers may store information outside Australia.

Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. We keep enquiry information only as long as it’s reasonably needed for its purpose, our business records and our legal obligations.

Access and correction

You can ask what personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. Email anthony@swarmintelligence.au. We may need to confirm your identity before responding.

Complaints

If you’re concerned about how we’ve handled your personal information, email us first and we’ll respond as soon as we can. If you’re not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au (opens in a new tab).

Changes to this policy

We’ll update this policy as the website and products change. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
