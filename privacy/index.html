Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

This policy explains how Swarm Intelligence Pty Ltd handles personal information through this website and across Swarm Labs products.

Website · Contact · Money Money Money · Defrazzle · Dosely · Your choices

This website

The website does not currently use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. Our hosting and network providers may process routine technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested pages, timestamps and security logs to deliver and protect the website.

If measurement tools are introduced, this policy will be updated. Swarm favours proportionate, privacy-aware measurement.

Contacting Swarm

If you email us, we receive the information you include, such as your name, email address, organisation and enquiry. We use it to respond, keep an appropriate business record and manage any resulting relationship. We do not sell contact information.

Money Money Money

Money Money Money is coming soon. The website does not collect financial information for the app. Product privacy information will be published alongside the release experience.

Defrazzle

Defrazzle is coming soon. It is designed around notices a person deliberately chooses to share, a strong preference for on-device processing and parent review before information is saved. The public website does not accept family notices.

Dosely

Dosely is coming soon and is designed as a local-only medication diary. Diary information is stored in the app’s private Application Support directory with iOS complete file protection. The product is designed without advertising or third-party analytics.

Users can export raw JSON, export a PDF report, enable optional Face ID, Touch ID or device-passcode access, and delete locally stored diary data. Once a user shares or saves an exported file outside Dosely, that copy is controlled by the selected destination and is no longer protected by Dosely’s application sandbox.

External links and affiliate links

Our site may link to external services. Their privacy practices apply once you leave this website. If affiliate links are used, they may include identifiers needed to attribute a referral. We will disclose the commercial relationship where it is relevant.

Storage and disclosure

We retain enquiry information only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose, business records and applicable legal obligations. We may use service providers to operate the website and business, or disclose information where required by law. We do not sell personal information.

Access, correction and questions

You may ask what personal information we hold about you, request correction, or raise a privacy concern. Contact anthony@swarmintelligence.au. We may need to verify your identity before responding to an access or correction request.

Changes

We may update this policy as the website and products evolve. The current date will appear at the top of this page. Material product privacy changes will be reflected alongside product updates.
