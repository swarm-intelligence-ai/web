About Swarm

Small by design.
Senior by default.

Swarm Intelligence is an Adelaide-based technology consultancy and product business. The work is personal, practical and grounded in the belief that people should be stronger after a transformation, not more dependent on an external provider.

Founded in 2008. Working across Australia in data, AI, architecture, analytics, digital services and product development.
