About

A small consultancy, on purpose.

Swarm Intelligence has been Anthony Paterson’s independent practice since 2008. It stays deliberately small and senior: the people in the conversation are the people doing the work.

01Story

Eighteen years of hands-on work.

Anthony founded Swarm Intelligence in Adelaide in 2008 to do hands-on data and analytics work for organisations that needed senior capability without a large firm around it. Over the following years that work grew from business intelligence into data strategy, platform architecture and transformation, across government, higher education, financial services and industry.

Between 2020 and 2026, Anthony co-founded and helped scale a specialist Data & AI consultancy as its Chief Operating Officer. That experience of growth, delivery at scale and team building now informs a practice that is small by design and senior by default.

Swarm Labs is the other half of the business. Anthony and Kym Paterson use it to research, build and test products for everyday problems, and to publish practical guides on the same subjects.

02People

The people in the conversation.

Anthony Paterson

Founder and Principal

Anthony is a data, AI and transformation leader with more than 25 years across strategy, architecture, engineering, operations and delivery. He started as a developer and business intelligence specialist, led the analytics function at a financial institution, and has since led data platform, governance and transformation work in government and higher education.

He moves comfortably between executive conversations and the detail of how systems, information and teams actually work. His strength is making complex problems understandable, finding a practical path forward, and staying close enough to delivery to make sure the thinking holds up.

Anthony studied computer and information science at the University of South Australia.

Kym Paterson

Product and Operations, Swarm Labs

Kym brings operational experience across government, customer service and education support, with a strong focus on understanding individual needs and improving how people experience services.

As an accredited Educational Support Officer, she works closely with young people and their learning needs, and brings that practical, people-centred perspective to Swarm Labs’ product research, testing and development.

03How we work

Four principles.

  • Clarity over complexity

    Make the problem understandable before proposing a solution.

  • Outcomes over activity

    Measure progress by decisions made and capability built, not documents produced.

  • Adoption over theory

    Design for the people who will run the result after the engagement ends.

  • Bring people with you

    Listen, explain the why and make room for people to contribute, so teams are stronger when we leave, not more dependent.
