The problem

Appointments ask for details memory cannot supply.

When a medication review arrives, it can be difficult to remember dose times, patterns and side effects across the previous weeks. Dosely turns user-entered observations into a clear history and a print-friendly PDF report.

  • Record a doseLog medication name, dose, date and time.
  • Check in clearlyCapture focus, mood, anxiety, appetite, crash, sleep, side effects and notes.
  • Review the historySee dose and check-in information in one understandable timeline.
  • Prepare a reportExport an appointment-ready PDF or the underlying JSON data.

Designed boundary

A diary and report.
Not medical advice.

Dosely does

Record user-entered information, generate neutral observations and produce clear exports.

Dosely does not

Recommend medication changes, diagnose a condition, check drug interactions or provide clinical decision-making.

Local-only by design.

The current MVP has no account, cloud database, advertising or third-party analytics. Diary data is stored in the app sandbox with iOS complete file protection, with optional Face ID, Touch ID or device-passcode access. Exported files are controlled by the destination the user chooses. Read the full current privacy statement.
