01What it does

What it does.

  • Record a dose

    Capture the medicine, dose, date and time in a few taps.

  • Check in

    Note focus, mood, appetite, sleep or anything else you’ve been asked to track.

  • See the history

    Doses and check-ins together in one clear timeline.

  • Prepare a report

    Create a clean PDF for your records or your next appointment.

02How it works

How it works.

  1. 01

    Record

    Log each dose as you take it.

  2. 02

    Check in

    Answer the same short prompts at regular times.

  3. 03

    Report

    Export a clear summary when you need it.

03Privacy

Private by design.

Your diary stays on your iPhone. Dosely is designed without advertising or third-party analytics, with optional Face ID, Touch ID or passcode protection, and exports you control. Dosely supports record-keeping; it doesn’t give medical advice, and you and your health professionals remain responsible for care decisions.
