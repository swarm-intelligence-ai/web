01What it does

What it does.

  • Know what’s yours to spend

    Bills, everyday spending and savings goals in one weekly view, so the balance you see is the balance you can use.

  • Turn goals into weekly steps

    Saving for a car, a trip or a buffer becomes a weekly amount with a visible finish line.

  • Learn through everyday choices

    Short, practical guidance at the moment it’s useful, not a course you have to finish.

  • See progress build

    Streaks, milestones and small rewards through Swarm Rewards make good habits visible.

02How it works

How it works.

  1. 01

    Plan

    Set your pay cycle, bills and one or two goals.

  2. 02

    Track

    Add spending in seconds as it happens.

  3. 03

    Save

    Move a weekly amount toward each goal.

  4. 04

    Learn

    Review the week and adjust what isn’t working.
