A calm, guided money app for young people learning how to budget, save and build confidence with money. With a friendly sidekick, simple tools, rewards and small incentives, it helps turn bills, spending and savings goals into everyday habits that feel achievable and encouraging.

The problem

Money advice can feel like a lecture.

Starting a first job, paying bills and saving for a car all arrive at once. Money Money Money turns those moving parts into a clear weekly view, practical goals and progress that feels encouraging rather than punitive.

  • Know what is yours to spendSeparate bills, goals and everyday money into a view that makes sense.
  • Build achievable goalsTurn a target into smaller weekly steps and see progress over time.
  • Learn by doingFriendly guidance, calculators and patterns help build confidence.
  • Make progress visibleStreaks, badges and small incentives recognise positive habits.
