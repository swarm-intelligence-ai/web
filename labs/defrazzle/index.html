01What it does

What it does.

  • Share it in seconds

    Paste text, or add a photo, screenshot or PDF.

  • Find the useful details

    Dates, locations, costs, consent, packing lists and links.

  • See what needs attention

    Deadlines and missing details are flagged, not guessed.

  • Check it, then save it

    Nothing joins the family plan until you’ve reviewed it.

02How it works

How it works.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Share a notice, email, screenshot or PDF.

  2. 02

    Review

    Check the extracted dates, actions and gaps.

  3. 03

    Plan

    Save confirmed items to the family plan, with an owner.

03Privacy

Private by design.

Defrazzle is designed around content you choose to share, a strong preference for on-device processing, and your review before anything is saved.
