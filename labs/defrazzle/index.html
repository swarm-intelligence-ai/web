The problem

The important bit is buried on page three.

Parents and carers receive school emails, PDFs, sport updates, screenshots and paper notices across too many places. Defrazzle finds the dates, actions and gaps, then asks the parent to confirm them before anything is saved.

  • Supply the notice deliberatelyPaste text or add a photo, screenshot or PDF. No whole-mailbox access.
  • Find the useful detailsEvents, dates, locations, costs, consent, preparation and missing information.
  • Trace every important factExtracted details stay connected to the original words.
  • Review before actionUnclear information is labelled. Nothing important is silently guessed.

Current product boundary

Focused on notices.
Not the whole household.

The current native slice supports on-device notice extraction and the first Notices, Calendar and Family views. The broader roadmap will only advance as real family testing proves what is useful.

Currently being tested

Structured plans from supplied notice text, parent review, action states and an organised per-child view.

Not being claimed yet

Automatic school integrations, payments, broad household management or unsupervised actions.

Private by design.
Parent in control.

Defrazzle is designed for deliberately supplied content, with a strong preference for on-device processing and a required human review before action. Read the current product privacy statement.
