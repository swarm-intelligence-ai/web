How we build

Small products.
Clear boundaries.

Swarm Labs is separate from client consulting. These products are not enterprise platforms placed inside client environments, and they do not create dependency on Swarm.

  • Start with a real jobEach product is designed around a specific everyday problem.
  • Keep people in controlClear review, understandable outputs and honest product boundaries.
  • Respect the informationPrivacy choices are designed into the product, not added as an afterthought.
