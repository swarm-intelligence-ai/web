Money Money Money

Launching October 2026

Learn it. Plan it. Own it. Money Money Money is the free budgeting app that gets your first job, first car and first real bills. Meet your helper, see what’s yours to spend each week and level up while you save.

  • Know what’s yours: your week, worked out, spent guilt-free
  • Save for the good stuff: goals, bills and rego, all planned
  • Get rewarded: level up and keep your streak, with real perks coming soon
  • No bank login, no fees, and your data stays on your phone

Platform: iPhone · For: young Australians

Defrazzle

Coming October 2026

Because apparently we’re expected to remember everything. Defrazzle turns the school notice you were about to lose in a group chat into a calendar event, a to-do and a nagging reminder that actually nags. Paste it, photograph it or just share it: the on-device AI does the reading so you don’t have to.

  • Share straight from Mail, paste it or snap a photo
  • Dates, costs, the packing list and the deadline, sorted per kid
  • Missing details flagged, not guessed
  • All on-device, all private

Platform: iPhone and iPad · For: busy families
