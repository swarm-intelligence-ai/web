02How we build

Four rules for every product.

  • Useful from the first minute

    One clear job and a short path to value, with no lengthy setup before it helps.

  • People stay in control

    You review what’s saved, decide what’s shared, and can export or delete your information.

  • Private by design

    We collect as little as possible and prefer processing on your device where it’s practical.

  • Plain language

    No jargon, no guilt and no manipulative design. Money, family and health are stressful enough.
