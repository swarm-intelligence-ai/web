04Standards

How we write guides.

  • Checked against official sources

    Every guide links to the official sources it relies on, such as Moneysmart, the Fair Work Ombudsman and healthdirect.

  • General information, clearly labelled

    Guides aren’t financial, legal or medical advice, and say so.

  • Independent of commercial relationships

    Partners don’t decide what we cover or conclude. Any affiliate link is disclosed on the page.

  • Reviewed and dated

    Each guide shows when it was last updated, and is reviewed when official guidance changes.
