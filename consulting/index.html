01Services

Four kinds of work, one senior team.

  • 01

    Data and AI strategy

    Strategies, roadmaps and investment choices that connect data and AI to real organisational outcomes.

    Typical outputs: data and AI strategy, prioritised roadmap, investment case, operating model options.

  • 02

    Architecture and information design

    Modern data platforms, integration and analytics designed around legacy systems, security, governance and the way people make decisions.

  • 03

    Transformation and delivery

    Hands-on leadership from concept through implementation, aligning executive intent, delivery teams and practical sequencing.

    Typical outputs: delivery plan and sequencing, transition from legacy platforms, delivery leadership, capability uplift.

  • 04

    Governance and assurance

    Clear decision rights, delivery controls and evidence that let teams move with confidence.

    Typical outputs: governance model, decision records, architecture assurance, data quality and review practices.

04Selected work

Built in the real world.

A selection of engagements, most recent first.

  1. 01

    SA Government transformation

    2026 to presentGovernmentData architecture and integration

    A major South Australian Government transformation program delivering complex digital services across legacy and on-premises environments, with significant security, governance and delivery dependencies.

    • Modern data architecture and integration patterns
    • Data platform, analytics, information and reporting architecture across Azure, Databricks, Fabric and Power BI
    • Legacy platform modernisation and transition planning
    • Architecture governance and design decision support

    Focus: pragmatic architecture that works within real operational constraints while creating a path to modern, reusable data capability.

  2. 02

    Specialist Data & AI consultancy

    2020 to 2026Co-founder and Chief Operating Officer

    A specialist Data & AI consultancy working on complex enterprise transformation across government, higher education and industry.

    • Led business operations and delivery strategy through a period of significant growth
    • Developed scalable delivery models, governance and internal capability
    • Worked with executives on transformation programs, aligning data, analytics and AI with operational priorities
    • Built and mentored cross-functional teams across engineering, analytics and delivery

    What it reinforced: successful transformation depends as much on clarity, operating discipline and adoption as on technology.

  3. 03

    Flinders University

    2019 to 2021Higher educationConsulting engagement

    The university’s Planning and Analytical Services team needed a modern cloud analytics environment and support for a more data-driven operating model.

    • Led and contributed to the design and implementation of a Microsoft cloud analytics platform using Azure, Databricks, Power BI and Power Platform
    • Delivered the Flinders Intelligence Portal (FLIP), a customer-facing analytics portal
    • Contributed to an enterprise data governance model and governance reporting
    • Automated reporting and forecasting for student enrolments, workload and revenue
    • Established secure development practices for analytics and uplifted team capability through training and mentoring

    Result: the university’s Power BI tenancy was migrated from Singapore to Australia with no disruption to business users.

  4. 04

    Indigenous Land and Sea Corporation

    2018 to 2019GovernmentAnalytics consulting

    The corporation needed a clear direction for its data and analytics capability and reporting.

    • Reviewed the analytics team and processes and identified future-state options
    • Developed a business intelligence strategy covering pain points, governance, architecture and a roadmap
    • Designed a business partner model for the data and analytics team
    • Supported the Tableau and Power BI evaluation, then configured the selected Power BI tenancy

    Result: a clear roadmap for the team, and a dashboard giving insight into key organisational performance indicators.

  5. 05

    People’s Choice Credit Union

    2012 to 2018Financial servicesConsulting, then Manager Analytics

    A member-owned financial institution that needed consistent enterprise reporting and, later, a stronger analytics function.

    • As principal BI consultant, delivered three major analytical reporting initiatives with consistent datasets, hierarchies and business rules
    • Established a consistent approach to report design, making complex information easier to use
    • As Manager Analytics, led business partners, BI developers, financial performance modellers and Dynamics 365 developers
    • Developed an enhanced analytics strategy covering self-service Power BI, advanced analytics, AI and cloud solutions

    Result: timelier, more useful performance reporting for portfolio and operational decisions.

Earlier work includes a data migration of client and insurance records for RAA through a successful go-live, and data and reporting work for the Department for Families and Communities, Masonic Homes, the Australian Wine and Brandy Corporation, Bendigo and Adelaide Bank and The Yalumba Wine Company.
