Design and governance framework

From evidence to insight people can understand, trust and act on.

The Swarm Insights Design & Governance Framework is a practical method for analytics and AI work. It combines information design, accessibility, human review and clear ownership, so insight is used rather than just produced.

Discuss your reporting
01The problem

Accurate isn’t the same as useful.

Reports that are correct but unused. Dashboards no one owns. AI outputs no one can explain or check. These are rarely technology failures. They happen when evidence, design, assurance and ownership are treated as separate projects, or skipped altogether.

02Four stages

Evidence, design, assure, govern.

  1. 01

    Evidence

    Start with the question the insight must answer, the data behind it and the context needed to interpret it honestly.

    Output: agreed questions, data sources and known limitations

  2. 02

    Design

    Shape the insight and interaction around the decision the audience needs to make, with accessibility built in from the start.

    Output: information design, prototypes and accessibility checks

  3. 03

    Assure

    Apply human review where the consequences warrant it, and record what was checked, by whom and against what.

    Output: review points, traceability and quality checks

  4. 04

    Govern

    Name the owner of each insight, data source and decision right, so accountability outlasts the project.

    Output: ownership, decision rights and a maintenance rhythm

03Where it applies

Wherever information informs a decision.

  • Analytics and reporting programs

    New dashboards and reporting suites designed around decisions from the outset.

  • AI-assisted insight

    Review, traceability and ownership for outputs generated or summarised by AI.

  • Public-sector reporting

    Where accessibility, transparency and accountability obligations apply.

  • Existing reports

    As a structured review of reporting already in use, with practical improvements.

04Using it

Part of the work, or a review on its own.

The framework is part of how Swarm delivers analytics and platform work. It can also be applied on its own as a review of existing reporting.

It pairs naturally with the data platform accelerator: one provides governed data, the other governs how that data becomes insight.
